Pricing FAQ

Before you buy

What is the difference between the 7-Day Pass and Lifetime?

Both unlock the same Pro features. The pass lasts 7 days from purchase and suits a single seasonal book or a deadline; nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel. Lifetime never expires and includes every future update.

What happens when my pass ends?

The Studio goes back to the free edition. You can keep building and previewing, and new exports carry the proof header and footer again. Books you exported while the pass was active stay clean and stay yours to sell.

Can I upgrade from a pass to Lifetime?

Yes. Buy Lifetime whenever you like and enter the new key; Gridbook keeps whichever license lasts longer. The pass is a separate purchase, so its price is not taken off Lifetime.

Is there a subscription?

No. Both paid options are one-time payments and nothing renews automatically.

Will the Lifetime price go up?

Yes, on a fixed date. The $29 launch price runs until January 31, 2027, and from February 1, 2027 Lifetime costs $39. There is no limit on the number of buyers. If you buy at the launch price, you keep every future update.

How do I get and use my license key?

Right after checkout, Gumroad emails your license key and shows it on your receipt page. In the Studio, open Unlock Pro, paste the key and press Activate. The check sends only your key, with the ID of the product it belongs to, to Gumroad.

How many computers can I use?

Your key works in up to 5 browsers or devices. Each browser counts once, the first time you activate the key in it, so a private window, a new browser profile or clearing this site’s data counts as a new one. If you reach the limit, email us and we will free up room.

What is the refund policy?

Ask within 30 days of your purchase and we refund it in full, for any reason. That includes the KDP promise: if KDP rejects your interior for a technical-spec reason, you get your money back and we fix the cause. Write from the email address you used at checkout; refunds go back to your original payment method through Gumroad. Before you buy, you can build and preview the whole book in the free edition.

What currency are the prices in?

US dollars. Gumroad handles checkout and shows the total, including any tax that applies where you live, before you pay.
