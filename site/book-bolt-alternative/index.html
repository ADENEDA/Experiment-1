When each one is the better choice

Every tool here is a reasonable pick for someone. This is who we think each one suits best.

  • Monthly toolkit

    Book Bolt

    • You want keyword and niche research in the same tool as your interiors. Gridbook has no research tools at all.
    • You design covers and interiors in a drag-and-drop editor with templates.
    • You make many kinds of low-content books besides puzzles, and a subscription suits the way you work.
    Source: 2025 review

  • One payment, word puzzles

    PuzzleBindery

    • You need crosswords, cryptograms, codewords, fill-ins, word scrambles or number search. Gridbook doesn’t make these yet.
    • You want a full-wrap cover designer, not just a guide.
    • You publish series and want one-click next volumes.
    Source: puzzlebindery.com

  • Pay per book, AI help

    KDP Builder

    • You want AI help with the whole package: research, the cover and the listing metadata.
    • You also make journals, coloring books or other book types.
    • You publish now and then, and prefer paying per book with credits that don’t expire.
    Source: kdpbuilder.com

  • Desktop, sudoku only

    Sudoku Maker

    • You publish only sudoku and want variants Gridbook doesn’t make: Diagonal, 16×16 Super Sudoku and Wordoku.
    • You want six difficulty levels and up to 500 puzzles in one book.
    • You prefer a desktop app for Windows or Mac.
    Source: puzzlebookmaker.com

  • Our product

    Gridbook Studio

    • You want picture nonograms: from the built-in library, your own picture or a drawing, each one solvable without guessing.
    • You want one low price: $29 for lifetime at launch, or $9 for a 7-day sprint.
    • You want KDP’s interior rules handled for you, and nothing uploaded.

What Gridbook Studio doesn’t do

  • No crosswords, cryptograms or other word puzzles yet: word search, sudoku, mazes and picture nonograms only.
  • No cover artwork. You get a free cover guide with the bleed, spine and safe zones marked, and design the cover yourself in Canva, Affinity or similar.
  • No keyword research, sales estimates or listing tools.
  • No AI writing. The puzzles are generated by algorithms and checked by solvers.
  • Sudoku comes in 4×4, 6×6 and 9×9 only.

Moving a puzzle book from another tool

Gridbook can’t import files from other tools, so a new volume means new puzzles. Match the trim size, puzzle size and difficulty of your earlier books so the series looks consistent. The margin calculator shows KDP’s minimum margins for your page count, and the spine width calculator gives the full cover size, so an existing cover design can be resized for the new page count.

The free edition builds and previews the whole book and exports proof PDFs with a “Free proof” header and footer, so you can check every page before paying anything.
