You own your books

Books, printables and project files you make are yours. We claim no rights in them and never receive a copy. You don’t have to credit Gridbook Studio in anything you sell. Pro can add an optional credit line to the copyright page, but it is off unless you turn it on.

Puzzles are generated from settings and a seed, so someone using the same settings and seed would get the same puzzle. That is why your books are yours to sell, but not exclusively yours: we can’t stop anyone else from making a similar puzzle.

The built-in word lists, pictures and fonts

Use them inside your puzzles as much as you like, in free printables and in Pro books you sell. What you may not do is take the content out and sell or share it on its own, for example as a pack of “word-search word lists” or a set of picture silhouettes.

  • Word lists: written for Gridbook Studio.
  • Nonogram pictures: silhouettes based on Phosphor Icons (MIT License), turned into puzzle grids. See Credits.
  • Font: Atkinson Hyperlegible (SIL Open Font License 1.1) is embedded in your PDFs. The font license explicitly does not apply to documents made with the font, so your books are unaffected. See Credits.

Your own pictures and word lists must be yours to use, or used with permission.

Free edition details

  • Printables are US Letter or A4, with up to 10 puzzles plus answers per file, and a “Free printable from gridbookstudio.com” footer.
  • Book interiors export as proofs, marked “Free proof” at the bottom and “Proof copy · not for sale” at the top of every page. KDP lists watermarks among the common reasons it rejects files, so proofs are for checking your design, not for upload.
  • PNG page images (300 DPI) and the ZIP bundle for KDP are part of Pro.
  • Classroom use includes photocopying printables for your students and posting them, free, where your students can download them.

Pro details

  • A license is for one person (you, or one person at your business), on up to 5 browsers or devices.
  • The 7-Day Pass runs for 7 days from the moment you buy it. Anything you export during those 7 days stays licensed for commercial use afterwards.
  • Already made a book in the free edition? Activate Pro, open the book and export it again: the new files are clean and covered by your license.
  • A refund cancels the license, including the right to sell what you exported with it. See the Refund policy.
