The 30-day refund

Lifetime and the 7-Day Pass both come with a 30-day refund. Email us within 30 days of your purchase and we will refund the full amount. You don’t need to give a reason, though we are grateful to hear what didn’t work, so we can fix it.

For the 7-Day Pass, the 30 days are also counted from the purchase, even though the pass itself lasts 7 days.

The KDP promise

We build interiors to KDP’s published paperback specifications and check every file before export. If KDP still rejects your Gridbook Studio interior for a technical-spec reason, email us within 30 days of your purchase for a full refund. Please include KDP’s message, so we can find the cause and fix it for everyone.

A technical-spec reason is a problem with the PDF file Gridbook Studio made, for example:

  • margins or the gutter too small for the page count;
  • text or lines too close to the trim or bleed edge;
  • fonts not embedded;
  • a page size that doesn’t match the trim size you chose, or a page count outside KDP’s limits;
  • lines too thin, or text too small, to print.

Some rejections are not about the interior file, and no software can prevent them: the cover, the title or description, KDP’s content rules (for example on low-content or duplicate books), trademarks in a listing, or account issues. The same goes for an interior that was edited after it left Gridbook Studio, and for free proofs, which carry a watermark and are not meant for upload. Within the 30 days you can still have your money back for any reason, so you are covered either way.

After the 30 days

If KDP rejects a Pro interior for a technical-spec reason later on, email us anyway. We will look into it, fix the problem as quickly as we can and help you get the book through review.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email hello@gridbookstudio.com, ideally from the address you used at checkout.
  2. Include your Gumroad receipt or order number, or the last 4 characters of your license key.
  3. For a KDP rejection, paste KDP’s message or attach a screenshot of it.

We reply within [OWNER: 2 business days]. Refunds are issued through Gumroad to your original payment method; how long it takes to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

What happens after a refund

  • The refunded license key stops unlocking Pro. The Studio re-checks keys about once a week, so Pro switches off on your devices at the next check.
  • A refund cancels the license, including the right to sell books or printables exported with it. Please don’t publish them.
  • Your books, project files and the free edition keep working as before.

Chargebacks

Please email us before you dispute a charge with your bank: a refund is quicker for you and for us. A key whose payment is charged back stops working, just like a refunded one.
