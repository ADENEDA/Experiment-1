What stays on your device

The Studio saves your work in your browser’s own storage on your device. We cannot see any of it. It stays until you delete it.

Every entry starts with gbs.. We set no cookies.

gbs.projects.index and gbs.project.… (local storage)
Your books: titles, settings, puzzle seeds, and your own word lists and nonogram pictures (stored as small grids). Removed when you delete a book in the Studio, or clear this site’s data.
gbs.prefs (local storage)
Studio preferences, such as the last open book, the view, the zoom level and the printable paper size. Removed when you clear this site’s data.
gbs.license (local storage)
Your license key or offline code, the plan (Lifetime or 7-Day Pass), when it was last checked, when a pass ends, and the device count reported by the payment provider. No name, email address or payment details. Removed when you press “Deactivate on this device” in the Unlock dialog, or clear this site’s data.
gbs.license.counted (local storage)
Short scrambled tags (hashes) of keys that this browser has already counted as a device, so activating again doesn’t use up another device. Not the keys themselves. Removed when you clear this site’s data.
gbs.session.freeBanner (session storage)
Whether the free-edition banner has already been shown in this tab. Removed when you close the tab.

The Studio also writes and immediately removes a test entry (gbs.__probe) to check that storage works. If your browser blocks storage, the Studio still works but cannot autosave; use Save project instead.

Pictures you turn into nonograms are read and converted in your browser; only the resulting grid is kept, inside your book. PDFs, PNGs, ZIP bundles and project files (.gridbook.json) are saved straight to your device as downloads.

What is sent, and to whom

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare receives the technical details of each request your browser makes: your IP address, the page or file requested, your browser’s user agent and the page that linked to it. Cloudflare uses them to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. We see only totals, not individual requests. [OWNER: change this if you host somewhere else.]

Page-view counts: Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see which pages are useful. A small script from static.cloudflareinsights.com reports each page view to Cloudflare: the page address, the referring page, your browser, operating system and device type, your country (worked out from your IP address) and how fast the page loaded. Cloudflare describes it as using no cookies or local storage and not fingerprinting visitors. We see only totals, such as visits per page and top referrers. The license check

When you press Activate in the Studio, it sends a request directly from your browser to Gumroad’s license API (api.gumroad.com) containing:

  • your license key;
  • the ID of the product the key may belong to (Lifetime first, then the 7-Day Pass, so at most two requests);
  • whether to count this browser as a new device.

About once a week, when you open the Studio online, it checks the key again in the same way, without counting a new device. No book content, files or pictures are ever sent.

Gumroad replies whether the key is valid, when it was bought, whether it was refunded or disputed, how many devices have used it, and some purchase details, such as the buyer’s email address. The Studio uses the validity, purchase date, refund status and device count, and keeps nothing else: it never stores your email address.

If we sell through Polar instead of Gumroad, the same check goes to Polar (api.polar.sh) with your key and our organization ID. Offline unlock codes are checked on your device and send nothing. Like any web request, these checks reach Gumroad or Polar with your IP address and browser details, and their own privacy policies apply.

Checkout

Where you can buy (the Pricing page, the pricing section of the home page and the Studio’s Unlock dialog), your browser loads Gumroad’s checkout script from gumroad.com, which in turn loads files from assets.gumroad.com, so that checkout can open on the page. On the home page it loads only when the buy buttons come near the screen, or when you point at or focus one. Other pages, including this one, and the rest of the Studio do not load it.

When you buy, you give your payment details to Gumroad, not to us, and Gumroad processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. Gumroad then gives us what any seller receives: your email address, your name if you gave one, your country, what you bought and when, and your license key. We never see your full card number.

We use buyers’ email addresses to deliver and support purchases (for example, to reset a key) and for important notices about Gridbook Studio, such as a change to these policies. Any product news we send has an unsubscribe link.
